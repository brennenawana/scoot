# Scoot

A tiny macOS menu bar buddy that reminds you to move.

Scoot lives quietly in your menu bar and nudges you to get up and move your body on a set interval — because sitting still all day is bad for your posture, your mood, and your brain. The nudge itself is the product: a sound, an overlay, or a little collectible buddy that pops up and dances in the corner of your screen.

Simple at the core, built for delight, designed to be extended.
