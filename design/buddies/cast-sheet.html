Launch cast — 12 species, one taste spectrum

The Pokémon spread: cute, cool, badass, and weird, so everyone finds theirs. All sprites below are the real committed sheets generated by scripts/gen-buddy-cast.py — 40×32 frames (20px art in a 2px lean apron, ×2 integer scale, nearest-neighbor). Rarity spread: 4 Common / 3 Uncommon / 2 Rare / 2 Epic / 1 Secret — the odds the app publishes are the odds the engine rolls (property-tested).
