The reveal window is still the front door — see design/surfaces/reveal-flow.html for the full six-beat storyboard (capsule → meet → name → done); it's not re-drawn here. What's new since that card was authored:

Copy quoted verbatim from CollectionPopoverSection.pitchCard/pitchStage — “Wilbur” and “11 more friends” are the field-evidence buddy from §3d (traffic-cone, first-ever roll) substituted into buddyName / remainingCount (12 species − 1 found). 36 words, one button, no drawn-out demo — the elevator pitch, not a walkthrough.

Got it → CollectionManager.dismissPitch() flips pitchDismissed once (write-once, mirrors a UserDefaults date) → stage derives to .done → the same frame that applies the icon also calls startSchedulerIfReady(), which starts the scheduler exactly once, right then → every hidden surface returns together: status line, Nudge Now/Pause/Resume, the divider, Scootdex, Settings, Quit. Nothing drip-feeds in — “only gate what's honestly empty” cuts both ways.

Design rationale (§3d): dopamine first, explanation after — the first thing a new user does is pull a lever and watch something land, not read a card. Progressive disclosure by omission, not by tooltip: Scootdex, Settings, the meter, even the full right-click menu don't get grayed-out or badged “locked” — they simply don't exist yet, because there's honestly nothing behind them (no collection, no history, nothing to configure that matters before you've met your first buddy). The transformation is the tutorial: no separate walkthrough teaches “this icon is your buddy,” the icon just becomes one, mid-glance, with a burst to make sure you're looking. And the hold is the quiet fix underneath all of it — an app that nudges you before you've pulled your first buddy is nudging on behalf of a promise it hasn't kept yet.

Funnel telemetry: onboarding_started (first arrival at “?”) and onboarding_completed (Got it) are registered in docs/CONTRACTS.md §8.5 — this is the first thing the friends & family round measures (BACKLOG §1/§3d), so every beat above is instrumented, not just the naming moment v0.2 already tracked.
