Tone rules

Meter copy counts up (“3 of 5”), never down. A skipped nudge simply doesn't advance it — no decay, no streaks, no “don't lose” language (banned words list, docs/PRODUCT.md §8). Multiple tickets stack quietly: “2 rolls ready” — still no urgency.

Placement: the meter lives between the countdown and the verbs — visible every time the popover opens, but the countdown stays the primary line: Scoot is a movement app first, a collection second. The ticket panel replaces the meter row when a roll is waiting (the meter keeps filling underneath for the next one).
